Accessibility statement

As part of the University of California, CDL is required to follow the University of California Information Technology Accessibility Policy to help ensure that as broad a population as possible may access, benefit from, and contribute to the University's electronic programs and services. The Policy requires that all web content in EZID complies with WCAG 2.1 Guidelines at level AA success criteria. Accordingly, users are responsible for ensuring that content they provide to EZID meets this standard.
